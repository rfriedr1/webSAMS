Every image file in this folder (sub-folders included) whose name starts with a sample number is shown on that sample's page — 48211.jpg, 48211b.jpg and 48211_back.png all belong to sample 48211. JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP and BMP are displayed; TIFF and HEIC are offered as downloads.

A photo of several samples appears on each of them: 25328-25337.jpg or the short form 11584-7.jpg (11584–11587) for a range, 16191_16192.jpg or 16881+16882.jpg for individual samples. A number after an underscore counts photos of one sample (36231_2.jpg).

The path as seen by the computer that runs webSAMS, not by your browser. On a Windows server use the network path (\\server\share\…) rather than a drive letter: mapped drives do not exist for a service. Leave empty to switch photos off.
